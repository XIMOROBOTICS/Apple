About Hidayat Academy

Preserving sacred knowledge, shaping future scholars — serving India since 1972.

Hidayat Academy is one of India's respected Islamic institutions, dedicated to preserving and transmitting classical Islamic knowledge through structured, rigorous, and authentic programs. Our faculty comprises graduates of leading Islamic universities in India — including Darul Uloom Deoband, Nadwatul Ulama Lucknow, and Aligarh Muslim University — along with certified Huffaz holding traditional Ijazah chains reaching back to the Prophet ﷺ.

Our Mission

To cultivate a generation of Indian Muslims grounded in the Quran and Sunnah, equipped with the tools of classical scholarship, and prepared to serve their communities with knowledge, character, and compassion — in the spirit of our nation's rich Islamic heritage.

Our Programs

  • Hifz Program — Full Quran memorization with Ijazah-track guidance and structured Dawr (revision) cycles.
  • Aalimiyyah — A 7-year scholar program following Dars-e-Nizami, covering Fiqh, Hadith, Tafsir, Aqeedah, and Arabic.
  • Arabic Intensive — Nahw, Sarf, and Balagha for serious students of the Arabic language.
  • Tajweed Certification — Advanced recitation rules and the sciences of Qira'at.

Why Choose Hidayat Academy

  • Recognized by State Waqf Board and affiliated with leading Islamic universities
  • Qualified Ulama with traditional Ijazah chains to Darul Uloom Deoband & Nadwa
  • Small class sizes with personal mentorship (Tarbiyat)
  • Separate hostel facilities for boys and girls from across India
  • Integrated focus on knowledge (ʿilm) and character (tarbiyah)
  • Scholarships available for economically weaker sections (EWS)
  • Free education and boarding for Hifz students

Our Campuses

  • Main Campus: Hyderabad, Telangana
  • Branch: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh
  • Branch: Bengaluru, Karnataka
  • Branch: Kozhikode, Kerala
